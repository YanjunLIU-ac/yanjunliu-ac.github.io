I am a Ph.D. student from [Department of Electronic Engineering](https://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/en-gb/), [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html), under the supervision of [Prof. Hongliang Ren](https://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/en-gb/people/academic-staff/professors/prof-ren-hongliang) and [Prof. Jiewen Lai](https://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/en-gb/people/academic-staff/professors/prof-lai-jiewen). 

My researches focus on the intersection of continuum robots, surigcal robots, human-robot interaction and robotic control systems. Specifically, I am developing continuum robot-assisted surgical procedures in ENT, OB/GYN, GI, Urology etc. For inquiries or collaboration, you can contact me via email [yanjunliu at link.cuhk.edu.hk](mailto:yanjunliu@link.cuhk.edu.hk).
